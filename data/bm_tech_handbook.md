# BM Technology: Comprehensive Company Policy Handbook

## 1. Hybrid Work & Office Operations
BM Technology supports a hybrid work environment. 
- **Office Days:** All employees are expected to be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
- **Remote Work:** Remote work is permitted on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays with manager approval. 
- **Core Hours:** Core hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST, during which all employees must be available for synchronous communication.
- **Desk Policy:** We utilize a hot-desking policy. Please clear your desk at the end of each day.

## 2. Data Security & Intellectual Property
- **Ownership:** All code, algorithms, models, and customer data are the sole property of BM Technology.
- **Production Access:** Access to the production database is strictly restricted to the Engineering Team.
- **Security Standards:** Employees must use mandatory two-factor authentication (2FA) for all company-provided software accounts (Slack, Jira, GitHub).
- **Zero Trust:** Sharing credentials, using personal USB drives for company work, or accessing internal networks via public Wi-Fi without a VPN is a violation of our Zero Trust Security Protocol.

## 3. Leave and Time Off
- **PTO:** Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year.
- **Request Process:** All PTO requests must be submitted via the HR Portal at least two weeks in advance.
- **Sick Leave:** Sick leave does not require prior approval but must be reported to your direct manager by 9:00 AM on the day of the absence.
- **Parental Leave:** BM Technology offers 12 weeks of paid parental leave for all employees after 6 months of tenure.

## 4. Code of Conduct & Culture
- **Inclusive Culture:** We foster an inclusive culture. Discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind will result in immediate disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
- **Transparency:** We emphasize radical transparency. If a mistake is made, report it immediately to your manager rather than attempting to hide it.
- **Conflict Resolution:** If you have a dispute with a colleague, attempt to resolve it directly first. If unsuccessful, escalate to your direct supervisor.

## 5. Performance & Growth
- **Reviews:** Performance reviews are conducted bi-annually (June and December).
- **Professional Development:** Every employee receives a $1,000 annual budget for courses, certifications, or conferences relevant to their role at BM Tech.
- **Growth:** We encourage internal mobility. Employees are eligible to apply for different internal teams after 12 months in their current position.

## 6. Equipment & IT Support
- **Hardware:** All employees receive a company-issued laptop, monitor, and noise-canceling headset.
- **IT Issues:** For hardware malfunctions, submit a ticket via the "IT Help" Slack channel. Replacement equipment is generally shipped within 48 hours.
- **Personal Devices:** Strictly no company proprietary software may be installed on personal devices.
